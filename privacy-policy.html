Privacy Policy

Effective May 6, 2026

Answer Easy ("we," "us," "our") provides AI-powered text messaging and call handling services on behalf of local businesses ("Clients"). This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our service via SMS, phone calls, or our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you text or call a phone number operated by our service, we collect:

  • Your phone number
  • The content of your text messages
  • Call metadata (time, duration, caller ID)
  • Voicemail recordings and transcriptions, if applicable
  • Appointment details you provide (name, preferred date/time, service requested)

When a business Client registers with us, we collect their business name, contact name, phone numbers, address, hours of operation, and service details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your text messages with relevant business information
  • Schedule appointments on your behalf
  • Forward urgent messages to the business owner
  • Send you a follow-up text if your call was missed
  • Send appointment reminders
  • Provide daily activity summaries to business Clients

3. SMS & Mobile Data

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number and message data are used solely to provide the messaging service you initiated by texting or calling the business.

Message frequency varies based on your interaction. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance.

4. Information Sharing

We share your information only with:

  • The specific business you contacted — they receive your messages, appointment details, and call summaries so they can serve you.
  • Service providers — we use Twilio for messaging, Anthropic for AI processing, and Google Calendar for scheduling. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies and data processing agreements.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

Conversation logs are retained for up to 90 days to maintain context for ongoing interactions. Appointment records are retained for the duration of the business Client's subscription. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) for all communications. Message logs are stored in encrypted databases with access restricted to authorized systems. We do not store payment card information.

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • Opt out of messages at any time by replying STOP
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Contact us with questions about this policy

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
